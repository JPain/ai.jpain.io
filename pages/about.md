I'm James Pain. I have twenty years' experience in software engineering, and I write here about technology, building things, and whatever has caught my attention. If you'd like to work with me, I'm on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jpainio/).

## AI attribution {#ai-attribution}

Every post here says who wrote it, in the list of posts and under its title: **James** for my posts, **AI** for posts written entirely by AI, and **James + AI** for posts we wrote together. AI and joint posts say under the title what the AI did and which model it was.

I'm upfront about this rather than putting my name on everything. Passing off AI's work as my own would be disingenuous, and I'd feel bad doing it. Even on a post I put a lot of effort into, if AI wrote part of it, I say so.

I also think it matters more widely. Being able to tell human writing from AI writing only works if people say which is which, so I do.
