I'm James Pain. I have twenty years' experience in software engineering, and I write here about technology, building things, and whatever has caught my attention. If you'd like to work with me, I'm on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jpainio/).

## AI attribution {#ai-attribution}

I think it's important that everybody is upfront about what content is AI-generated and what is human-generated, for humanity's sake. Posts that have been written with AI will be attributed as such in the byline and will have a notice at the top of the post explaining what parts AI was responsible for.
